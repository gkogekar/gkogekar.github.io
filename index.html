About

I am a computational engineer focused on CFD, thermal-fluid systems, and multiphysics modeling. My work involves developing numerical models for transport phenomena, reacting flows, and reactor systems, with an emphasis on physical accuracy, robustness, and computational efficiency.

I am interested in combining classical CFD methods with data-driven approaches to improve simulation workflows and support engineering design and decision-making in complex thermal and fluid systems.

I also have experience in STEM mentoring and supporting learners in mathematics and engineering fundamentals.
